RULES
One word, one capital. Reservedge. Never ReservEdge, never Reserve Edge, never all caps outside a mono eyebrow label.
The bracket always opens right. It holds the dot back; mirrored, it reads as releasing it.
Wordmark is Schibsted Grotesk 700 at -0.03em tracking. No other weight, no italic, no outline.
The gradient is for the icon only. Inside the product the mark sits on flat coral or ink.
Never place the mark on amber, green or danger fills. Those colours carry status meaning and the mark would inherit it.
No tagline in the lockup. If a line is needed it sits separately, in --ws-ink-2, at body size.
